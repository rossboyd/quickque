Quickque Remote

Starting local connection…

Scan the QR code in Quickque on your Mac, then approve the phone on the Mac. No app, account, installation, or internet connection is needed.

Connection help

Keep both devices on the same reachable local network. Your Mac can use Ethernet while your phone uses Wi-Fi. Allow Quickque through the Mac firewall and any local-network permission prompt. Guest Wi-Fi or client isolation can prevent devices from reaching each other.

Keep the Mac awake with Quickque’s reader open. If the Mac changes network or address, open a new QR session on the Mac and scan again. Use a current Safari, Chrome, or Firefox browser with JavaScript and browser storage enabled; an in-app camera browser may need “Open in browser.”

This connection uses unencrypted local HTTP. Use only a trusted LAN, not shared or hostile Wi-Fi. Script text, audio, transcripts, models, and library content stay on the Mac; only reader status and control commands are exchanged.
